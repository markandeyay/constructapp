## Browser walk, 2026-10-08, local stack, real Chrome

All four capabilities worked in a browser.

1. Plasmid: prompt "a yeast shuttle vector with URA3 selection and centromere
   maintenance" retrieved pRS416 (0.854), rendered a 4,898 bp map with 7
   features. The overall verdict was FAIL (11 percent GC window, lac promoter
   region not compatible with the yeast host). The flow works; the verdict is
   not a green one. `01_plasmid.jpg`.
2. AAV: lacZ with CAG gave 5,229 bp, FAIL, and the message names CAG to EFS,
   saving 1,427 bp (`02a_aav_before_cag_fail.jpg`). With EFS and WPRE unchecked:
   3,802 bp, packaging PASS, overall WARN on Kozak only (`02b_aav_after_efs_warn.jpg`).
3. Assembly: the gold pair `assembly.good.a1.gibson_two_fragments`, Gibson,
   overall PASS, 15 checks, 4 primers. `03_assembly_pass.jpg`.
4. Guide RNA: pUC19 beta-lactamase target, SpCas9, Knockout. 10 of 216 guides
   shown, PASS, 10 checks, off-target banner names 950 bp and says it is not
   genome-wide. `04_grna_ranked.jpg`.

Findings the presenter must know:

* The AAV form has "Include WPRE if it fits" CHECKED by default. With it
  checked the EFS cassette is 4,391 bp (a 589 bp WPRE fits), not 3,802 bp. The
  runbook says to untick it. Setting the box programmatically did not take
  effect in the React state; a real click did.
* There is no hand-off from the AAV result to the assembly tab. Fragments are
  pasted.
* The plasmid demo prompt returns FAIL on the retrieved pRS416 record.
* During the walk the page once reloaded and dropped form state (dev server).
