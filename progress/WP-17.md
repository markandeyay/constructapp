# WP-17: the Kozak initiation context element, deferred since WP-03, now composed

Model: Opus for the ruling, the measurement and the review; Sonnet for the
mechanical implementation and for the gold and documentation edits, each
reviewed line by line against measurements taken here.

Closes the one remaining scientific item in the backlog, recorded twice: in
`PROGRESS.md` under `## Spec challenges` and in `progress/WP-03.md` under
`### Not done, and why`. WP-10 section 3 is the review that raised it.

## What changed, in one line

The AAV composer now places a 6 bp Kozak initiation context element between the
promoter and the coding sequence when the transgene supplies no 5' context of
its own, so position -3 of every composed cassette is a cited base rather than
an accident of where a curator drew a feature boundary.

## Why this was deferred, and why the deferral has expired

WP-03 recorded the reasoning and it was sound at the time. The sourcing argument
was already settled: `GCCRCC` is a cited constant in
`packages/validation/aav/constants.py` carrying its Kozak 1987 reference, and
instantiating the `R` as the preferred purine is ordinary vector design rather
than fabrication. What held it back was that it changes the bases of every
exported cassette and moves documented demo numbers, two days before a demo, and
that the behaviour in the meantime was honest: it warned, and the message named
the element the offending base came from and how to fix it.

That reason has expired. The demo has happened. The numbers it moves are
re-measured here against the running system rather than recalculated by hand.

## The defect, stated precisely

WP-10 measured the 3' end of every element that can sit immediately 5' of the
CDS and found that three of eight promoters carry a purine at -3, all three by
coincidence, and that `intron.chimeric` ends in a T at -3 and therefore sets the
position for any cassette including it whichever promoter was chosen. The base
at -3 carried no information about translation initiation. It was the last but
two base of whatever part the curator's feature boundary ended on.

The validator was right and the generator was wrong. That is the correct way
round for this to fail, and the response to a validator catching your generator
is to fix the generator, not to relax the check. Nothing about check 11 moved in
this work: not its accepted bases, not its threshold, not its severity. The test
that pins the WARN on a pyrimidine at -3,
`tests/aav/test_checks.py::test_pyrimidine_at_minus_three_warns`, passes
unmodified.

## What the element is, and why it is not an invented sequence

`KOZAK_UPSTREAM_ELEMENT` is not written as a literal anywhere. It is derived:

```python
KOZAK_UPSTREAM_ELEMENT = KOZAK_CONSENSUS_MOTIF[:6].replace("R", KOZAK_PREFERRED_MINUS3)
```

It is therefore positions -6 through -1 of the already cited consensus, with the
purine instantiated, and it cannot drift from that motif because it is computed
from it. Its length, 6 bp, is the extent of the 5' portion of the cited motif
and not a number chosen here. `KOZAK_ELEMENT_RULE` is interpolated from the same
two constants for the same reason.

`KOZAK_PREFERRED_MINUS3` is `A`. The check accepts either purine and its
threshold is identical for both, so this constant only decides which purine to
WRITE when a context is being designed from scratch. It moves no bar. The
check's own PASS message already said as much, in the sentence WP-03 added:
A and G both satisfy the consensus, and A is the commoner of the two in the
cited analysis.

The three new constants are deliberately NOT mirrored as `AAVThresholds` fields
and are NOT in `AAVThresholds.as_mapping`. They describe what the generator
writes, not what the validator accepts, and putting them in `parameters_used`
would imply the check's bar had become configurable through them.

## Four decisions worth defending

### 1. Its own role, not bases prepended to the CDS

`CassetteElementRole.KOZAK` is an addition to the section 6.2 list, which names
the functional order and does not enumerate an initiation context element.
Logged below as a spec challenge. Four grounds:

* The element sits immediately 5' of the CDS, so it inverts none of the
  relations section 6.2 fixes. `FUNCTIONAL_ORDER` was renumbered to give it rank
  4, between `INTRON` and `CDS`, and `aav.element_order` needs a rank for it or
  it raises on lookup.
* The CDS element's bases must stay exactly the coding sequence, because
  `aav.cds_integrity` reads them for the ATG, the frame and the stop. Prefixing
  six bases to that element would have broken check 6 to fix check 11.
* Section 11.1 requires every base to be attributable. Absorbing six bases into
  the user's transgene element would attribute them to the user, who did not
  supply them.
* The length budget, the GenBank feature table and `AAVDesign.notes` all key off
  the role, so a role is what makes the element visible in all three. The
  record's requirement was explicit: a silently inserted element is not
  acceptable.

It is absent from `REQUIRED_ROLES`, because a transgene carrying its own 5'
context yields a correct cassette without it, and absent from `OPTIONAL_ROLES`,
because the remediation engine must never offer to remove it to save 6 bp.
`ROLE_PART_CATEGORY` is now deliberately not total over the enum, since there is
no registry category for a rule derived element; `SUBSTITUTABLE_ROLES` gates the
only lookup, which was verified in source rather than assumed.

### 2. The insertion condition is a fact, not a heuristic

The element is inserted if and only if the resolved transgene begins with `ATG`.

A transgene beginning with its initiator codon supplies no 5' context, which is
the case the element is for. A transgene not beginning with `ATG` has whatever
precedes its `ATG` as context the caller supplied; prefixing a Kozak element
there would place it six or more bases from the initiator, where it does nothing
and where it would misrepresent the construct. That caller's context is honoured
verbatim instead. A transgene beginning with neither is a malformed coding
sequence, which `aav.cds_integrity` reports; the composer does not try to repair
it.

There is no new request field and none was added: section 6.3 is reproduced
field for field and `AAVRequest` forbids extras. The escape hatch for a caller
who wants a specific 5' context is to supply it on the transgene, which is how
anyone preserving a native 5' UTR would supply it anyway.

Both branches leave a note. The element is never inserted silently and never
omitted silently.

### 3. The 6 bp is in the WPRE fit gate

`base_total`, which decides whether WPRE is admitted, now includes the element.
Getting this wrong by 6 bp would have let a WPRE through that takes the cassette
over the target, which is the one arithmetic error in this change that would
have mattered. A test pins it at the exact boundary: with EFS, SV40 and two
AAV2 ITRs the fixed overhead is 637 bp and WPRE is 589 bp, so a 3,471 bp
transgene gives 4,697 bp without the element and 4,703 bp with it, against the
4,700 bp target. WPRE is dropped.

### 4. The provenance claim is verified, not trusted

The element's bases are the fourth origin of section 11.1, the one WP-08 added:
a published rule. The attribution adapter emits a `PUBLISHED_RULE` span naming
its rule, which is what `AttributedSegment` requires of that origin, and the
rule text reaches the design's provenance and the audit entry.

The first implementation trusted the element: any `KOZAK` role element with a
source got a span attributing its bases to Kozak 1987. That was wrong, and it
contradicted this module's own promise that the registry path "looks the id up
in `data/parts` and compares the bases, so the claim is verified rather than
taken on trust." The adapter now emits the span only when the element's bases
are the bases the rule produces. A `KOZAK` element carrying anything else leaves
its span uncovered, which blocks the export and names the bases, and a test
pins that with a six base element of `TTTTTT`.

`classify_source` was left alone. It recognises three prefixes on purpose, and
widening it would have loosened the gate for every capability to serve one.

## What this does NOT do, stated because it would be easy to overclaim

**It does not remove the Kozak warning, and composed cassettes do not now pass
check 11.** Check 11 requires a purine at -3 AND a G at +4. The element fixes
-3. Position +4 is the first base of the transgene's second codon, which the
composer cannot change and must not change, because that is the user's protein.

Measured on the demo payload: `docs/demo_assets/transgene_lacZ_JF300162.1.txt`
is 3,075 bp, begins `ATGACC`, and its fourth base is A. So the demo cassette
still reports `aav.kozak_context` WARN. What changed is what the warning is
about and what it advises:

| | Before | After, measured |
|---|---|---|
| Context window | `ACACAGATGG` style, set by the promoter part | `GCCACCATGA` |
| Severity | WARN | WARN |
| Strength wording | weak, or adequate, depending on the promoter | adequate but not optimal |
| Reason | -3 was a curator's feature boundary, plus +4 | +4 alone |
| Advice | insert a `GCCRCC` prefix before the ATG | change the second codon, synonymously |

The old advice was correct before and is wrong now, because the prefix is
already there. The WARN message's prefix sentence is therefore emitted only when
-3 is what fails. That is the one message change in the validator, and it
removes advice that would otherwise have been impossible to act on.

Sixteen of the 32 AAV gold cases are built from a request and therefore go
through the composer. Of those sixteen, eleven now PASS check 11, where before
they passed only when a promoter's feature boundary happened to end on a purine,
and five still WARN, every one of them on +4 alone because their payload's
second codon does not begin with G. That is the honest shape of this fix: the
tool stopped contributing the defect and now reports only the part of it the
user owns.

## What it changes in exported DNA, measured

Every composed cassette grows by exactly 6 bp and its `design_id` changes, since
the id is a hash of the cassette. Measured against the running system, not
calculated:

| Scenario | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| CAG, bGH, lacZ, no WPRE | 5,229 bp FAIL | **5,235 bp** FAIL, 14 checks |
| over target / soft limit / hard ceiling | 529 / 329 / 29 | **535 / 335 / 35** |
| EFS, bGH, lacZ, no WPRE | 3,802 bp WARN | **3,808 bp** WARN, 14 checks |
| EFS, bGH, lacZ, WPRE on | 4,391 bp | **4,397 bp** |

The band figures are untouched: 4,700 target, 4,900 soft limit, 5,200 hard
ceiling. The CAG to EFS saving is still 1,427 bp, because it is a difference of
two part lengths. lacZ is still 3,075 bp. The check count is still 14.

The GenBank feature carries the citation with it:

```
     regulatory      358..363
                     /label="Kozak initiation context"
                     /note="translation initiation context element, Kozak
                     consensus GCCRCCATGG, 5' of the start codon"
                     /note="Kozak 1987 vertebrate translation initiation
                     consensus, positions -6 to -1 of GCCRCCATGG with the purine
                     at -3 instantiated as A (Nucleic Acids Research 1987,
                     15:8125-8148, doi:10.1093/nar/15.20.8125)"
                     /construct_role="kozak"
                     /regulatory_class="ribosome_binding_site"
                     /construct_part_id="none"
                     /construct_source="published_rule:kozak_1987"
```

`ribosome_binding_site` is the closest INSDC `regulatory_class` controlled term,
not an exact one: the vocabulary has no `kozak` class, and the comment above the
mapping says so rather than implying a match.

The length budget names the element and its origin. The `part` column was
widened from 22 to 30 characters and now falls back to the element's source
token, because the old fallback printed "user supplied" for a row the user did
not supply:

```
 3  Kozak initiation context   published_rule:kozak_1987            6     1,790     2,910
 4  lacZ coding sequence       user_input:transgene_sequence    3,075     4,865      -165
```

## The one gold case regraded, justified individually

`aav.good.b3.authentic_cmv_promoter` declared WARNs on `aav.internal_repeats`
and `aav.kozak_context`. The second no longer fires and has been removed. The
`aav.internal_repeats` entry is untouched.

This is a regrade of the construct, not of the bar. That case's own recorded
justification said the only way to score a purine at -3 "would be to insert
synthetic linker bases between the promoter and the start codon". The composer
now does exactly that, with the cited element, so -3 is an A instead of the T
the CMV part's curated feature boundary ended on, and this case's payload
already carries G at +4. Measured: `-3 is A, +4 is G, context GCCACCATGG`, PASS.
A WARN would now misreport the molecule.

No other case's `expected_warnings` changed. Every other AAV case was
re-measured and reports exactly the set it declares. Prose in several cases was
corrected where a figure moved by 6 bp or where the biology it described is no
longer what happens, in particular
`aav.bad.neuron_promoter_with_liver_target`, whose justification attributed the
-3 base to `promoter.hsyn1` and can no longer do so, and
`aav.good.b1.native_initiation_context_preserved`, whose justification carried a
genuine biological error from the start: it claimed inserting a `GCCACC` linker
"changes the second codon of the protein". Six bases 5' of the ATG change no
codon at all. The real tradeoff, and the reason that case still warns, is that
+4 can only be fixed in the payload's own second codon.

`b1`'s case id is now a partial misnomer. A payload beginning with `ATG` has no
5' context to preserve; what the case preserves is the native second codon and
therefore the native +4. The id was NOT renamed, because it is referenced
elsewhere in the build record, and the rationale now says what the case actually
demonstrates.

## Spec challenge logged

Section 6.2 fixes the cassette order and does not enumerate a translation
initiation context element, so `CassetteElementRole.KOZAK` is an addition to
that list. The grounds are in decision 1 above. It reorders nothing section 6.2
fixes and adds no check: section 6.4's "These are the checks. Do not add an
invented one" is untouched, and there are still exactly fourteen.

## Deliberately not done

* **Check 11 was not relaxed.** Section 3.4 cuts both ways and a validator
  quietly loosened to make an output clean is worse than one reporting an
  inconvenient truth. Not one accepted base, threshold or severity moved.
* **No request field was added.** Section 6.3 is reproduced field for field, so
  there is no `include_kozak` flag. The transgene's own 5' context is the
  control, and it is a fact about the input rather than a switch.
* **`classify_source` was not widened.** Adding a `published_rule:` prefix there
  would have loosened the attribution gate for all four capabilities to serve
  one. The AAV adapter special cases the role and verifies the bases instead.
* **No historical figure in `PROGRESS.md` or `progress/WP-*.md` was rewritten.**
  Those record what was measured when, and the build record is append only. The
  superseding figures are in the appended WP-17 summary.
* **`apps/web/e2e/fixtures/aav_over.json` and `aav_fixed.json` were not
  touched**, by instruction: another worker owns that directory. They are static
  mocked API responses carrying pre-change totals and no Kozak element. They
  remain self consistent with their own spec file, so the e2e suite still passes
  against them, but they no longer match what the real API returns. Reported to
  the operator rather than edited.
* **`docs/demo_evidence/02a_aav_before_cag_fail.jpg` and
  `02b_aav_after_efs_warn.jpg`** are screenshots showing the pre-change totals.
  They cannot be corrected as text and were not regenerated. Reported.

## Still true, and not claimed otherwise

Nothing here makes a claim about whether a 6 bp consensus element measurably
raises expression for any particular transgene. The claim is narrower and is the
one the sources support: the construct now carries the published consensus at
the position the cited work identifies as decisive, instead of a base that was
there by accident, and the element is disclosed with its citation everywhere the
cassette is described.

The element only reaches cassettes the composer builds. The other sixteen AAV
gold cases supply a complete `AAVDesign`, and not one of them changed by a
single base. That is correct rather than a gap: `AAVDesign` is deliberately
permissive so it can hold a cassette that is wrong, which is what the checks
exist to reject.
